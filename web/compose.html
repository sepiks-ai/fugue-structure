Write a Fugue

Describe the fugue you would like: a key, a mood, how many voices, how fast. It is composed here in your browser, on an entry plan taken from one of Bach's fugues in the Well-Tempered Clavier.

Try:

SubjectSubject invertedFree counterpoint

How it works: your words set the key, mode, number of voices, speed and the character of the subject. A short theme (the subject) is composed to match. The plan of one of Bach's fugues decides when each voice states it, at which pitch and whether upside down; the program then writes the free counterpoint around those entries, quaver by quaver, under rules of harmony and voice-leading. The architecture is Bach's and the music in between is the machine's, which is where the difference lies. More in the article, on the Fugue Map, and in the code.
